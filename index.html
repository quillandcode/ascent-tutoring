Welcome to Ascent Tutoring

Learning something new can feel like staring up at a tall ladder—overwhelming, maybe even a little intimidating. But here's the thing: you don't climb a ladder all at once. You take it one rung at a time.

That's exactly how my tutoring sessions work. We start with the foundation—the first rung—and make sure it's solid before we move up. Each concept builds naturally on the last. No rushing, no skipping steps, no shaky footing. By the time we reach the top, what seemed complicated at the start suddenly feels clear and manageable.

🪜 The Skill Ladder Method

Every session is designed like a ladder. Each rung represents a key skill or concept that supports the next one. We practice, we reinforce, we make sure you're confident before climbing higher. And when you finally reach the summit? The view is crystal clear—you've got it.

Whether it's math, computer science, or problem-solving strategies, we'll build the path together. One solid step at a time.
