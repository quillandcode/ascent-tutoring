Algebra Cheat Sheet

Equations & Inequalities
⚖️ The Golden Rule
Whatever you do to one side of the "River" (the = or inequality sign), you MUST do to the other side to keep it balanced.
🎯 The 3-Step Success Plan
1 Simplify First: Distribute (multiply into parentheses) and Combine Like Terms on the same side of the river.
2 Pick a Side: If you have x on both sides, move the smaller one across the "river" to join the larger one.
3 Unwrap the Gift: Undo addition/subtraction first, then undo multiplication/division last.
⚠️ The Danger Zones
🌊 1. The "Flip Trap" (The River Analogy)

When you multiply or divide by a NEGATIVE number, the relationship is reversed.

The Analogy: Multiplying or dividing by a negative is so powerful it makes the River change direction.

Example: -4x < 20
Action: Divide both sides by -4... The river flips! → x > -5
📤 2. Reversing Division (x is on Top)

If x is being divided, use multiplication to set it free.

Example: x / 11 = 3
Action: Multiply both sides by 11. → x = 33
🏠 3. The "Basement" Problem (x is on Bottom)

If x is in the denominator, you must move it out of the basement first!

Example: 12 / x = 4
Action: Multiply both sides by x to get 12 = 4x.
Solve: Divide by 4. Result: 3 = x
